    No. 01

    A retrieval experiment

    This tool uses word embeddings to search a vector database for the most relevant course slides about a topic.

    Find the slide where I already explained it

    A technical project on one question: how do you match a question to a slide? Every slide I have taught since Fall 2023, 2,801 of them across five courses, is indexed four ways: what I said while it was on screen, an AI's description of its picture, its own text and speaker notes, and plain keywords. Each result shows which way found it and why. I use it to pull old slides into new decks.

    ask it the way
    a student would

    Each slide gets a meaning rank and a keyword rank; its score is 1/(60 + meaning rank) plus the keyword weight × 1/(60 + keyword rank). The default 0.5× is measured: keywords alone put the right slide first for 1 of 28 test questions, so they count half, but they still decide exact terms like SMOTE. 0× ignores keywords; 1× is equal weight; above 1× keywords lead.

    No. 02

    The experiment

    Four ways a slide can match a question

    Every slide is stored as several searchable items. A question is embedded once and compared with all of them by meaning; a keyword search runs over the same text. A slide ranks by its best item, and opening it shows which item won, its distance, and the sentence that matched.

    Narrationwhat I said while it was on screen

    Instructor lines from the class recording, cut into 120-word chunks, each its own item. Often the closest to how a question is asked, and stored per term, so the same slide keeps what I said in each course.

    425 pages of text
    Image descriptionwritten by a vision model

    Claude Haiku looks at the slide and writes what the picture shows, so a chart or a photo with no words on it can still be found by what it is about.

    every slide with a picture
    Slide text + notesthe deck itself

    Course, session, slide number, title, the text on the page and the first 220 words of the speaker notes, in one paragraph.

    all 2,801 slides
    KeywordsPostgres full-text search

    Every item's text is stemmed and indexed, so a slide's keywords are the words on it, in its notes, in its description and in what I said. Every word of the question must appear. By default a keyword hit counts half as much as a meaning hit; the slider above the questions moves that.
