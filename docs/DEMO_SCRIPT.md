# Demo video script (about 2 minutes)

Record https://slide-finder.vercel.app in a normal window. Don't show the passcode. Say what you
are doing as you go; the graders use the video to check the requirements.

1. **Open the app, log in.** (5 s)

2. **A query with no shared words.** Click the example "it fits the data it has seen perfectly
   and falls apart on anything new". Point out: the top slide is "The sweet spot, a balance of
   bias and variance", distance 0.50, matched through "what I said" (the transcript), and none
   of the words in the question are on the slide. Say: a keyword search returns nothing for this.
   (25 s)

3. **Open the slide.** Click it. Show the full slide, the "What I said" block with the time it
   was on screen, and "What is indexed for this slide": the card (slide text + notes + the
   description written from the image) and the transcript chunk. (20 s)

4. **Add something at runtime.** In the note box type "good example for the MBA elective" and
   click Add note. Close the slide. Search "MBA elective" and show the same slide comes back,
   flagged "My note". Say: added while the program runs, searchable immediately. (25 s)

5. **Do something with the results.** Open two or three slides from different courses and terms
   (try "how do I group customers into meaningful segments": Data Mining and AI for Business
   both have segmentation slides). Click "Pick for new deck" on each. Open Picked, show the
   list, click Export PDF, open the downloaded PDF. Say: this is how I start a new deck from old
   material. (30 s)

6. **An off-topic question.** Search "who won the Stanley Cup in 2019". Show the "I don't think
   I taught this" banner and the distance (0.60). (10 s)

7. **Repeats across terms.** Search "what's wrong with this picture" (Data Visualization). Show
   "used in 11 sessions" on the card and the term list in the slide view. (10 s)

8. **Persistence.** Reload the page: the note and the picks are still there. (5 s)

Mention once: Supabase Postgres with pgvector, Voyage embeddings, FastAPI on Vercel; about
N unique slides from five courses and every term since 2023; code and README on GitHub.
